      102 -- **A1.** All timestamps are treated as UTC and reduced to cal
          -endar dates; windows are whole-day counts.                    
      103 -- **A2.** 'Within N days' is inclusive: 0 <= procedure_date - 
          -item_date <= N. Day 30 passes, day 31 fails; day 14 passes, da
          -y 15 fails.                                                   
      104 -- **A3.** Items dated after the procedure date are not pre-ope
          -rative and are excluded; the most recent qualifying item on or
          - before the procedure date is used.                           
      105 -- **A4.** Procedure date: the structured field wins; if null, 
          -a date explicitly stated as the scheduled/target procedure dat
          -e in the documents is used only when every stated date agrees,
          - no document says the date is still TBD/pending, and the date 
          -is not earlier than the document stating it. Otherwise -> unkn
          -own -> MISSING_REQUIRED_DATA, and the window checks that depen
          -d on it are folded into that single issue.                    
      106 -- **A5.** Procedure risk comes only from the structured field.
          - Missing or not LOW/MODERATE/HIGH -> MISSING_REQUIRED_DATA. Te
          -sting checks still FAIL when they fail under every possible ri
          -sk (e.g. no CBC at all) and PASS only when they pass under eve
          -ry risk.                                                      
      107 -- **A6.** Rule 4 review time is metadata.submission_received_a
          -t (falls back to the latest vital). Readings are not discarded
          - for age; the most recent reading per vital type is used.     
      108 -- **A7.** Vitals come from the structured list and from free t
          -ext (nursing/intake notes, H&P exam lines). 'See flowsheet', d
          -eferred vitals and patient-reported home logs are not readings

          - if the note states one.

          -share the same time, the more abnormal reading wins (conservat

          -ature) -> unknown -> MISSING_REQUIRED_DATA, never READY.

          -he planned procedure. A signed consent is not invalidated by a
